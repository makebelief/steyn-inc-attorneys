PROPERTY LAW · GUIDE

Before a Property Transfer

Property transfers involve several documents and steps.

Before signing, confirm the property description, sale conditions, transfer process, the conveyancer’s role and anticipated costs.

A consultation can clarify which documents and instructions will be required in your transaction.

Contact a legal practitioner for advice tailored to your circumstances.

CONTRACTS · GUIDE

Reviewing a Commercial Agreement

A contract should reflect the parties’ actual understanding.

Commercial contracts should clearly set out performance, payment, liability, termination and dispute-resolution arrangements.

Consider professional review before signing, especially where important assets or ongoing obligations are involved.
