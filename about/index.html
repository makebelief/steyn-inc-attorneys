WHO WE ARE

A Gqeberha law firm with a clear focus.

Steyn Inc opened on 1 February 2022. Its director, Shawn Steyn, brings an established background in practice, with a focus on legal assistance that is practical and attentive.

The firm supports private individuals and businesses in property, conveyancing, civil disputes, contracts, sectional-title transactions and administration of deceased estates.

Our goal is not to overwhelm clients with technical language. It is to understand what matters, set out the available legal steps and work diligently toward an appropriate resolution.
